Data Job Market Analysis in Python

What do 785,741 data-job postings from 2023 say about hiring and salaries? Cleaned, analysed, and charted with Pandas and Matplotlib.

Objective. Turn a large, messy public dataset of data-job postings into clear answers: which roles are posted most, what they pay, and how postings change through the year.
Approach. Converted posting dates to a proper date type, dropped an unused column and rows with missing salaries, then grouped salaries by job title and country (median, min, max). Built monthly time-series counts and charted the results with Matplotlib.
Result. A clean, analysis-ready dataset, salary comparisons by role, and four charts covering posting volume, seasonality, and salary distribution.
